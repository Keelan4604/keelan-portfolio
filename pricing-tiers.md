## Sales Strategy Notes

### Upsell Path

The natural progression for every client:

```
GBP Optimization ($199)  -->  Professional Website ($500-2k)  -->  Chatbot Bundle (+$149/mo)
```

- **GBP is the door-opener.** Low price, fast delivery, immediate visible results. Builds trust.
- **Website is the core product.** Highest margin, most repeatable. Push this once GBP trust is established.
- **Chatbot bundle is the recurring revenue play.** Only pitch to businesses that clearly need lead capture or after-hours support (restaurants, contractors, medical offices, etc.).

### Payment Terms

- 50% upfront, 50% on delivery for all project-based work
- Monthly services (chatbot, maintenance) billed at the start of each month
- Accept Venmo, Zelle, or invoice via email

### Guarantee

- All tiers include a **30-day satisfaction guarantee**
- If the client isn't happy, we revise until they are or refund the balance
- This removes risk from the buyer's side and makes closing easier

### Ideal Targets

- Local service businesses (contractors, cleaners, landscapers, salons, restaurants)
- Businesses with no website or a clearly outdated one
- Businesses with incomplete or unoptimized Google Business Profiles
- Anyone already paying for Google Ads but sending traffic to a bad site

## Closing Tips

1. **Always start with the demo.** Show them what their site COULD look like. The demo sells itself.
2. **Anchor high.** Mention the Full Package first, then work down. Even if they buy Tier 2A, it feels like a deal.
3. **The GBP offer opens doors.** If they say "I don't need a website," offer the $199 GBP optimization. Everyone wants more Google calls.
4. **One closed job pays for the site.** A $500 website for an HVAC company that averages $5,000/job is a no-brainer. Frame it that way.
5. **After-hours pitch for chatbot.** Pull up their Google listing, show the phone number, ask: "What happens when someone calls at 9 PM?"
6. **Founding pricing creates urgency.** "I'm building my portfolio — the first 5 businesses get these rates locked in permanently."
7. **Bundle discount is real.** Full Package ($2,997 + $249/mo) vs. buying each separately ($199 + $1,500 + $149/mo + SEO). Show the math.
