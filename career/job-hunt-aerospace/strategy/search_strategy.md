## Decision rule
Apply quickly when role matches at least 3 of these:
- within Raleigh driving distance
- aerospace / aircraft / flight hardware
- test / validation / integration / systems
- entry-level, associate, intern, or 0-3 years
- tools overlap: MATLAB, Python, CAD, embedded, hardware test

Skip roles that are mostly:
- pure software with no hardware angle
- civil-only or manufacturing-only with no technical upside
- unrelated technician work without engineering growth
- too far from Raleigh to support the grad-school plan
