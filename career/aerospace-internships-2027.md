## Government & National Labs

### NASA Programs

#### NASA Pathways Internship Program
- **Locations:** All NASA centers (JSC Houston, KSC Florida, JPL Pasadena, GSFC Maryland, MSFC Huntsville, etc.)
- **Program:** Paid internship, direct pipeline to full-time GS position after graduation
- **Pay:** GS-5 to GS-9 scale (~$20-35/hr depending on education level)
- **Apply:** Applications posted on USAJOBS.gov; two windows per year (~February and ~September)
- **Requirements:** US citizen, currently enrolled, 3.0+ GPA
- **URL:** https://www.nasa.gov/careers/pathways/
- **Tips:** Create USAJOBS profile NOW. Set up notifications for "NASA Pathways." Federal applications require detailed resumes (longer than private sector). Apply to EVERY center you'd work at.
- **Competitiveness:** Very competitive. Tailor your resume to each specific posting using keywords from the announcement.

#### NASA OSSI (One Stop Shopping Initiative)
- **Locations:** All NASA centers
- **Program:** 10-16 week internships, summer/fall/spring
- **Pay:** Stipend-based (~$1,100/week for graduate students)
- **Apply:** Rolling; summer deadline typically ~February
- **URL:** https://intern.nasa.gov/
- **Tips:** Apply to multiple projects. Write compelling essays connecting your skills to specific NASA missions.

#### JPL Summer Internship Program
- **Locations:** Pasadena CA (JPL campus)
- **Program:** 10-week summer internship, paired with scientist/engineer mentor
- **Pay:** Monthly stipend + housing/travel allowance if >50 miles from JPL
- **Apply:** October 2026 - March 13, 2027 (but apply as early as possible)
- **Requirements:** US citizen or permanent resident, 3.0+ GPA, currently enrolled
- **URL:** https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/edu/internships/apply/jpl-summer-internship-program/
- **Competitiveness:** Highly competitive. Research the specific projects/mentors you'd want to work with.

## Fellowships & Grants

### NSF Graduate Research Fellowship Program (GRFP)
- **Award:** $37,000/year stipend + $16,000 cost-of-education allowance for 3 years
- **Eligibility:** US citizen/permanent resident, first-year graduate students only. Must be research-based MS.
- **Deadline:** Mid-October 2026 (for engineering disciplines)
- **Note:** Can only apply in first year of grad school. EXTREMELY prestigious. Aerospace engineering is an eligible field.
- **URL:** https://www.nsfgrfp.org/
- **Tips:** Start writing research and personal statements in summer 2026. UMich has GRFP workshops. Talk to your advisor about research framing.
- **Important:** If your MS is coursework-only (not thesis/research-based), you may not be eligible. Confirm with NSF.

### DoD SMART Scholarship
- **Award:** Full tuition + $25,000-$46,000/year stipend + summer internships at DoD facilities + guaranteed DoD civilian job after graduation
- **Eligibility:** US citizen (or Five Eyes), 3.0+ GPA, 1.5+ years remaining in degree
- **Deadline:** First Friday of December 2026 (opens August 1)
- **Commitment:** 1 year of DoD civilian employment per year of scholarship
- **URL:** https://www.smartscholarship.org/
- **Tips:** This is one of the best deals in graduate STEM funding. Apply early. The service commitment is at a DoD lab of your choice. Perfect for someone interested in defense aerospace.

### NASA NSTGRO (Space Technology Graduate Research Opportunities)
- **Award:** Up to $80,000/year (tuition + stipend) + summer research at a NASA center
- **Eligibility:** US citizen/permanent resident pursuing MS or PhD in STEM
- **Deadline:** ~Late January 2027 (solicitation drops ~December 2026)
- **URL:** https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-space-technology-graduate-research-opportunities-nstgro/
- **Tips:** Requires a research proposal aligned with NASA Space Technology priorities. Work with your advisor to craft this. Two UMich aero students won NSTGRO in 2024 - the department has a track record.

### AIAA Foundation Graduate Awards
- **Awards:** Multiple named awards ranging from $1,000-$9,000
  - Abe M. Zarem Award (aeronautics + astronautics tracks)
  - Dr. Hassan A. Hassan Award in Aerospace Engineering ($9,000)
  - Martin Summerfield Propellants & Combustion Award ($1,500)
  - Gordon C. Oates Air Breathing Propulsion Award ($1,000)
- **Eligibility:** AIAA student member, completed 1+ semester of graduate work, approved research topic
- **Application:** October 15, 2026 - January 31, 2027
- **URL:** https://aiaa.org/get-involved/university-students/undergraduate-scholarships-graduate-awards/
- **Tips:** Join AIAA immediately upon arriving at UMich. The Hassan and propulsion awards align perfectly with your interests.

### Rackham Graduate School Fellowships (UMich Internal)
- **Rackham Merit Fellowship (RMF):** Nominated by your department at admission (you don't apply directly). Collaborative funding package.
- **Rackham Predoctoral Fellowship:** For doctoral candidates (if you continue to PhD).
- **Other Rackham Funding:** $50M+ distributed annually to graduate students.
- **URL:** https://rackham.umich.edu/funding/
- **Tips:** Ask the aero department about RMF nominations during the admissions process. Many MS students get partial funding through GRA/GSI positions.

### Other Fellowships to Explore
- **NDSEG (National Defense Science & Engineering Graduate Fellowship):** Full tuition + $38,400/year stipend. DoD-funded. Highly competitive.
- **Hertz Foundation Fellowship:** For applied physical sciences. Very elite ($250K+ total value).
- **NPSC (National Physical Science Consortium):** Partners with national labs and DoD.
- **Tau Beta Pi Graduate Fellowship:** $10,000 for one year. Must be TBP member.
- **Amelia Earhart Fellowship (Zonta International):** $10,000 for women in aerospace-related science/engineering.

## Application Strategy

### Resume Tips for Aerospace Grad Internships
1. **Lead with education** - UMich MS Aerospace + UA BS Aerospace is a strong combination
2. **Technical skills section** - Prominently feature: CFD, FEA, MATLAB, Simulink, SolidWorks, CATIA, ANSYS, Python, C++
3. **Quantify everything** - "Reduced mesh convergence time by 40%" > "Performed CFD analysis"
4. **Projects matter** - Include senior design, research projects, personal projects
5. **One page** for most companies; federal resumes (NASA, DoD) should be 2-4 pages with full detail
6. **Tailor for each company** - SpaceX wants hands-on builders; Boeing wants process-oriented engineers; NASA wants researchers

### Interview Prep
- **Technical:** Be ready for fluids (Navier-Stokes, boundary layers, shock waves), structures (stress analysis, buckling, FEA theory), dynamics (orbital mechanics, stability), and coding (Python, MATLAB)
- **Behavioral:** STAR method. Prepare 5-6 stories covering teamwork, leadership, technical problem-solving, failure/learning
- **SpaceX-specific:** Expect rapid-fire technical questions and "what have you built?" type questions
- **Defense companies:** Expect questions about why you want to work in defense, handling classified information

### Networking Strategy
1. **LinkedIn:** Connect with UMich aero alumni at target companies. Personalized messages > generic connection requests.
2. **Career fairs:** Most valuable recruiting event. Arrive early for top companies.
3. **AIAA conferences:** Regional and national. Great for meeting engineers and hiring managers.
4. **Professor introductions:** The single most effective networking channel. Build relationships with faculty first semester.
5. **Referrals:** A referral from a current employee at SpaceX/Boeing/etc. dramatically increases your odds. Cultivate these through alumni networks.

### Priority Application Order (Based on Competitiveness + Fit)
1. **August 2026:** DoD SMART Scholarship, Boeing
2. **September 2026:** SpaceX, Blue Origin, Lockheed Martin, Northrop Grumman, GE Aerospace, RTX
3. **October 2026:** NSF GRFP, AIAA Awards, Rocket Lab, Relativity Space, L3Harris, BAE, AFRL
4. **November 2026:** JPL, NASA Pathways, Sandia, Los Alamos, APL, Aerojet Rocketdyne
5. **December 2026:** SMART deadline, start NSTGRO app
6. **January 2027:** NSTGRO deadline, AIAA deadline, Winter career fair, remaining positions

### Key Principle
> **Apply early, apply broadly, follow up relentlessly.** The best internships fill by November. Waiting until January means you're competing for leftovers. Target 20-30 applications minimum across government, defense, and commercial space.
