## 1. Joaquim R. R. A. Martins (MDO Lab)

**Email:** jrram@umich.edu
**Title:** Pauline M. Sherman Collegiate Professor of Aerospace Engineering
**Lab:** Multidisciplinary Design Optimization Laboratory (MDO Lab)
**Office:** FXB 3032

### Research Summary
Martins heads the MDO Lab, which develops computational frameworks for high-fidelity multidisciplinary design optimization coupling CFD with structural FEA. Recent work (2025-2026) includes geometrically nonlinear aerostructural optimization for highly flexible wings, coupled aeropropulsive design optimization of podded electric propulsors, liquid hydrogen tank boil-off modeling, and CFD-based hydrokinetic turbine design optimization. The lab develops open-source tools (OpenMDAO, MACH-Aero) used widely in the field. Author of *Engineering Design Optimization* textbook.

**Lab:** Computational Fluid Dynamics Group (CFDG)
**Office:** FXB 3012

## Notes

- **Timing:** Send these emails in April-May 2026, well before arriving in the fall. Earlier is better — funding decisions are made ahead of time.
- **Follow-up:** If no response in 10 days, send a brief follow-up. Professors get hundreds of emails.
- **Attachments:** Attach your resume/CV to each email. Do not attach it unsolicited to the first email — mention you can provide it.
- **Subject lines:** Keep them specific. "Incoming MS Student" signals you are already admitted, which matters.
- **Length:** These are all under 200 words. Respect their time.
- **Key differentiator:** Your eVTOL capstone + propeller acoustics research gives you a story that connects CFD + structures + applied design. Use it.
