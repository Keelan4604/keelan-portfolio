## 1. Tutoring Platforms

### Wyzant (BEST for starting)
- **Commission:** 25% platform fee (you keep 75%)
- **MATLAB tutor rates observed:**
  - Low end: $36/hr (Tyler K., BSE CompE from UMich, 48 reviews)
  - Mid range: $60-80/hr (most common for MATLAB)
  - High end: $120-195/hr (PhD-level, 800+ reviews)
- **Top MATLAB tutors on Wyzant:**
  - Gavin M. -- $100/hr, 1,891 reviews, ME background, control systems
  - Nina M. -- $75/hr, 2,706 reviews, PhD CompMath, control theory
  - Jason L. -- $120/hr, 836 reviews, physicist, data analysis
  - Matt J. -- $80/hr, 1,730 reviews, PhD Neuroscience
  - Andrew P. -- $75/hr, 2,299 reviews, 27-year MATLAB user
- **Signup:** Free to create profile. Set your own rate. Students find you via search. Good Fit Guarantee protects first sessions.
- **Competition:** ~25 top-rated MATLAB tutors. Few specialize in aerospace/Simulink specifically. HUGE gap for aerospace-focused MATLAB.
- **Recommendation:** Start at $55-65/hr, raise to $80+ after 20-30 reviews.

### Chegg Tutors
- **Pay:** ~$20/hr flat rate for live tutoring. $1-5 per written answer.
- **Commission:** Chegg sets the rate (you don't choose).
- **Demand:** MATLAB is listed as a "gap" subject -- fewer tutors available than demand requires.
- **Signup:** Apply online, take subject competency exam.
- **Verdict:** LOW pay. Use only for volume/review building if desperate. Not worth it at $20/hr.

### Tutor.com
- **Pay:** $21-37/hr estimated (math tutors). MATLAB-specific rate unclear but likely mid-range.
- **Requirements:** Must be at least a college sophomore, US resident, 5+ hrs/week availability.
- **Signup:** Online application, competency exam, 1-3 week process.
- **Verdict:** MODERATE. Decent supplemental income but not the best $/hr.

### Varsity Tutors
- **Student cost:** $70-100/hr depending on package.
- **Tutor pay:** Reported $15-25/hr (Varsity keeps the rest). Very unfavorable split.
- **Signup:** Apply online, background check, subject interview.
- **Verdict:** LOW pay relative to student cost. Avoid unless you need guaranteed client flow.

### Preply
- **Rates:** Tutors set own rates, typically $15-50/hr.
- **Commission:** 33% on first lesson with each student, then decreasing to 18%.
- **Verdict:** Better for languages than technical subjects. Low MATLAB demand.

### Superprof
- **Model:** Students pay $49/mo subscription + tutor fee. Tutors set own rates.
- **Commission:** None on lessons after first free lesson.
- **Verdict:** MODERATE. Less established in US for technical subjects. Worth a profile but not primary.

### Platform Ranking (for MATLAB tutoring):
1. **Wyzant** -- Best pay, most control, good MATLAB demand
2. **Tutor.com** -- Decent supplemental income
3. **Superprof** -- Free to list, zero commission model
4. **Chegg** -- High volume but terrible pay
5. **Varsity Tutors** -- Bad tutor pay despite high student cost
6. **Preply** -- Not ideal for technical subjects

## 2. Freelance Platforms

### Upwork
- **MATLAB jobs available:** ~123 open at any time
- **Simulink jobs available:** ~18 open at any time
- **Typical project types:**
  - Control system design (PID, state-space)
  - Simulink model building for physical systems
  - 6-DoF flight simulations (directly relevant to aerospace!)
  - Signal/image processing
  - Machine learning algorithm implementation
  - Data analysis and visualization
- **Pay rates:**
  - Entry-level: $35-60/hr
  - Mid-level: $60-120/hr
  - Senior/expert: $120-250/hr
  - Fixed-price projects: $50-5,000+ depending on scope
- **Commission:** 20% under $500/client, 10% for $500-10K, 5% over $10K
- **Key insight:** 6-DoF simulations, flight dynamics, and control systems are premium niches with fewer qualified freelancers. Keelan's aerospace background is a direct competitive advantage.

### Fiverr
- **MATLAB Simulink gigs:** 24+ active listings
- **Pricing observed:**
  - Bottom: $10-25 (offshore sellers, basic tasks)
  - Mid: $50-150 (project-based)
  - Top sellers: $200-500+ for complex projects
- **Commission:** 20% on all orders
- **Key categories with gigs:** matlab-simulink, matlab-coding, matlab-simulation, matlab-gui, simulink
- **Verdict:** Good for productized services (e.g., "I will build your Simulink model for $X"). Race to bottom on price for generic work, but aerospace-specific gigs can command premium.

### Freelancer.com
- **Volume:** Claims 12M+ MATLAB-related jobs historically
- **Rating:** MATLAB experts rated 4.9/5 from 15,891 reviews
- **Pay:** Generally lower than Upwork. More price-sensitive clients.
- **Commission:** 10% or $5 minimum
- **Verdict:** MODERATE volume but clients tend to be more budget-conscious.

### Other Platforms
- **Toptal:** Elite network, $100-200+/hr but rigorous screening. Worth applying once established.
- **PeoplePerHour:** Smaller market, some MATLAB/Simulink projects available.
- **Contra:** Commission-free freelancing platform, growing MATLAB presence.

### Freelance Platform Ranking:
1. **Upwork** -- Most MATLAB jobs, best pay, aerospace-relevant projects
2. **Fiverr** -- Good for productized gigs, build passive income
3. **Freelancer.com** -- Volume play, lower rates
4. **Toptal** -- Aspirational, apply after building portfolio

## 3. UMich-Specific Opportunity

### Student Population
- UMich has ~34,000 undergrads total
- College of Engineering is one of the largest in the US (~12,000+ students)
- Nearly ALL engineering undergrads take ENGR 101 (MATLAB + C++) -- hundreds of students per semester
- Multiple departments require MATLAB-heavy courses:
  - AEROSP 423: Computational Methods (MATLAB required)
  - ME courses: Controls, dynamics, vibrations
  - EECS: Signal processing, controls
  - CEE, BME, IOE: Various computational courses

### Campus Tutoring Infrastructure
- **ECAS (Engineering Center for Academic Success):** Hires tutors for ENGR 101 and EECS courses. Positions fill quickly. Apply for Winter/Fall terms.
- **Science Learning Center (SLC):** Accepting Fall 2026 tutor applications (interviews March 2026).
- **Tau Beta Pi / HKN:** Honor societies run drop-in tutoring for intro courses.
- **MATLAB access:** UMich has campus-wide MATLAB license (all students have access = all students need help).

### GSI/Tutor Pay at UMich
- **GSI hourly equivalent:** $42.10/hr (2025-2026 contractual rate)
- **GSRA stipend:** ~$29,192/term for CoE
- **Private tutoring alternative:** Can easily charge $50-80/hr privately, exceeding GSI rates with fewer hours.

### Untapped Demand
- ECAS tutoring is limited to specific courses and hours
- No dedicated MATLAB tutoring center exists
- Reddit/forums show constant requests for MATLAB help around midterms/finals
- Uloop (umich.uloop.com) lists MATLAB tutors -- sparse competition
- Word-of-mouth in grad student networks is powerful

### Hourly vs. Project-Based
- **Tutoring:** Hourly makes sense. Students budget per session.
- **Freelance projects:** Fixed-price is often better. Scope the work, price it, deliver. Avoids hourly tracking headaches.
- **Recommendation:** Do BOTH. Tutoring for steady income, freelance for bigger paydays.

### Package Deals
- **5-session intro pack:** 5 x 1hr sessions for $250 ($50/hr -- discount incentivizes commitment)
- **Midterm crash course:** 3 x 1.5hr sessions for $200 (~$44/hr, high conversion around exam time)
- **Semester support:** 10 sessions for $450 ($45/hr -- loyalty discount, guaranteed recurring revenue)
- **ENGR 101 survival pack:** 4 sessions covering MATLAB fundamentals for $180

### Differentiation Strategy
Keelan's unique positioning:
1. **Aerospace-specific MATLAB** -- flight dynamics, orbital mechanics, control systems, CFD post-processing
2. **Simulink expertise** -- most tutors know MATLAB scripting but NOT Simulink modeling
3. **Fellow grad student** -- relatable, knows the courses, knows the professors
4. **Real engineering applications** -- can show how MATLAB is used in actual aerospace work
5. **UMich insider** -- knows the curriculum, can tailor help to specific course assignments

## 7. Key Insights & Risks

### Why This Is a Strong Opportunity
1. **Supply gap:** Few MATLAB tutors specialize in aerospace/Simulink
2. **Built-in demand:** Thousands of UMich engineering students need MATLAB help every semester
3. **Seasonal spikes:** Massive demand during midterms and finals (2-3x normal)
4. **Low startup cost:** $0 to create profiles, just time investment
5. **Scalable:** Can move from platforms to direct clients, raise rates with reputation
6. **Resume building:** "Tutored 50+ engineering students" looks great

### Risks & Mitigations
- **Time vs. MS coursework:** Start at 5 hrs/week, scale only if manageable
- **Academic integrity concerns:** Be clear you teach concepts, don't do homework for students
- **Platform dependency:** Build direct client relationships ASAP to avoid 25% fees
- **Seasonality:** Demand drops in summer. Use summers for freelance project work instead.
- **AI competition:** ChatGPT/Copilot can help with basic MATLAB. Differentiate with Simulink expertise, debugging complex models, and conceptual teaching that AI can't replicate.
