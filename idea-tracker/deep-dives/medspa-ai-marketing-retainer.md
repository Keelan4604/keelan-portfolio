# Deep Dive: Med Spa Done-For-You AI Marketing + Membership Retainer

**Score: 74** | Evaluated: 2026-04-10 (Evening Session)

---

## What It Is

A done-for-you monthly retainer service for small Alabama med spas (~5 staff, 1-2 providers). You deliver:
1. AI chatbot on their website (repurposed SmartChat) for FAQ + appointment booking
2. Instagram DM automation (ManyChat or custom) that captures leads and routes to booking link
3. Monthly content package: 4 AI-generated before/after case studies formatted for Instagram + website
4. Quarterly GBP optimization

Pitched as a complete "digital growth system" not a collection of services.

## Target Profile

**Ideal client:** Alabama med spa with:
- 4.5+ star Google reviews (quality service, just no digital marketing system)
- Facebook/Instagram page with 500-5,000 followers but no chatbot or DM automation
- No website or Wix/Squarespace site (website rebuild upsell available)
- 2-10 staff, solo or 2-provider operation
- Services: Botox, fillers, laser, facials, body contouring (high LTV per client)

**Search method:**
- Google Maps: "med spa near Tuscaloosa AL", "medical spa near Birmingham AL"
- Look for: high star rating, lots of reviews, BUT no live chat, booking button, or automation visible on site
- Yelp: "BEST 10 Medical Spas Birmingham AL" — scan for weak websites/no chatbot

## First Steps (Ordered by Speed to Dollar)

1. **Find 5 targets on Google Maps / Yelp** — med spas with good reviews, no chatbot, basic website
2. **Audit their digital presence** — note: no DM automation, no booking chatbot, no case study content
3. **Build one industry-specific SmartChat demo** (med spa: "What services do you offer? Book a consult.")
4. **Cold call or walk in** (don't email — med spa owners are busy, phone/in-person works better)
5. **Pitch: "We installed a booking system for [similar local spa] and they added X appointments last month. Want to see it?"**

## Risks

- Regulatory: Alabama medical regulations require physician oversight for some treatments — positioning must be marketing-only, not clinical
- Seasonality: Med spas have seasonal peaks (pre-summer, pre-holidays) — use this in pitch
- Long sell cycle: Owner may need to "talk to their partner" — have a leave-behind one-pager
