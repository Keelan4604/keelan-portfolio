## Demand Evidence

**Real need, diffuse signal.** Alabama has significant manufacturing across:
- Automotive (Mercedes-Benz in Vance, Toyota in Huntsville, Honda in Lincoln)
- Aerospace/defense (Boeing, Lockheed Martin, and 800+ aerospace suppliers in Huntsville/Decatur corridor)

Small manufacturers (10-200 employees) commonly lack professional technical documentation. They run on tribal knowledge, lose it when employees leave, and face audit failures when they can't produce documented procedures. ISO 9001 certification — increasingly required for Tier 2/3 auto and aerospace suppliers — requires documented SOPs.

**What they currently pay:** Professional technical writers charge $75-150/hr. A full SOP package (10-15 procedures) typically costs $3,000-8,000 from a professional firm. Safety plan development: $1,500-4,000. ISO 9001 gap assessment + documentation support: $5,000-20,000.

**Weak direct evidence:** No Reddit posts or Upwork listings found specifically for "Alabama manufacturer SOP writing." This is a cold-call/relationship market, not an inbound one — which means less competition for anyone willing to do outreach.
