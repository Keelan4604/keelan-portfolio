## What It Is

Write government bid responses for small Alabama trade contractors (HVAC, electrical, paving, roofing, plumbing) who want to bid on city, county, and state contracts but lack the time, skills, or knowledge to compete. Keelan uses Claude to rapidly parse RFPs, identify requirements, and produce compliant, competitive proposal responses — with engineering background lending technical credibility.

**Important distinction from federal contracting:** This is NOT competing with Sweetspot, AutogenAI, or ProposalAI. Those are enterprise tools for defense contractors bidding on $10M+ federal contracts. This is for a local HVAC company wanting to win the City of Tuscaloosa's HVAC maintenance contract, or a paving company bidding on a county road project. Totally different market.

**Alabama APEX Accelerator** (formerly PTAC) exists specifically because small contractors CAN'T navigate procurement alone. They offer free consulting but are resource-constrained and don't write proposals themselves.

But the paving, electrical, HVAC, and landscaping operators are exactly who wins municipal maintenance contracts.

- Owner-operated trade contractors with 5-50 employees
- Want to add government contracts to their revenue (stable, often multi-year)
- Have the capability to do the work but find RFP documentation intimidating

## Current Pricing and Competitors

**What people currently pay:**
- Proposal consultants on Upwork: $50-150/hr for proposal writing (often not local or familiar with Alabama)
- APEX Accelerator: FREE (but they advise, they don't write)
- Large proposal management firms: $5,000-15,000+ per proposal (only for big contracts)

**Gap:** No one is offering affordable, fast-turnaround bid writing for small Alabama contractors on $25K-$500K municipal contracts. The free options (APEX) are underresourced. The paid options price out small contractors.

**What Keelan charges:**
- $300-600 per bid response (simple/routine bids)
- $800-1,500 per complex bid (multi-year maintenance contracts with technical specs)
- Monthly retainer: $500-1,000/mo for active bidders (receive all relevant RFPs, Keelan submits responses)

## AI Leverage Analysis

Claude's specific advantages:
- **Parse a 40-page RFP in minutes**, extracting all requirements into a checklist
- **Generate a compliance matrix** (every requirement → how we address it)
- **Draft proposal sections** based on contractor's capabilities and past work
- **Format to exact specification** (many municipal bids have strict formatting requirements)
- **Research comparable past bids** to inform competitive pricing strategy

Time without AI: 15-30 hours per complex bid response. With Claude: 3-6 hours. **5-8x leverage.**

Keelan's engineering background = reads technical specs accurately. A generic VA or copywriter would miss technical nuances in specs (e.g., HVAC tonnage requirements, electrical load specs, material grade specs for paving).

## Honest Assessment

**Worth pursuing?** YES — but with realistic expectations.

**Pros:**
- Genuinely underserved at the local execution level
- Per-project pricing = fast cash, no long sales cycle
- Engineering background = real credibility for technical bids
- Recurring: contractors who win once want to bid again

**Cons:**
- Outbound heavy — contractors don't search for this
- APEX is a free competitor in the market
- Some RFPs have bonding/insurance requirements the contractor needs (Keelan can't help with that)
- Won't scale massively — cap is probably 5-10 active clients

**Bottom line:** Could realistically generate $1,500-3,000/month with 3-4 active clients. Not a unicorn, but notably less saturated than most AI service ideas and uses Keelan's engineering background as a genuine moat.
