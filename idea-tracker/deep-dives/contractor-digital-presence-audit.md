## What It Is

Deliver a paid, comprehensive audit of a local contractor's entire digital presence — not just their website, but Google Business Profile, reviews, site speed, spam/security issues, local SEO ranking, and competitor gap analysis. The audit is a paid product ($200-350), not a free lead magnet. It creates urgency (documented problems), credibility (professional report), and a clear path to implementation (which Keelan then sells).

**Why charge for the audit?** Free audits scream "sales pitch." A $250 paid audit positions Keelan as a consultant, not a vendor. And it surfaces problems so clearly that the contractor sells themselves on the fix.

## Audit Components (Claude-assisted)

**1. Website Technical Audit**
- Site speed (PageSpeed Insights — free API)
- Mobile responsiveness
- Security (HTTP vs HTTPS, spam injection check)
- Broken links / 404s / error pages
- Contact form functionality
- Missing CTAs

**2. Google Business Profile Audit**
- Profile completeness (photos, hours, description)
- Review response rate
- Category optimization
- Q&A section populated?
- Posts recency

**3. Local SEO Snapshot**
- Where do they rank for their primary keyword in their city?
- NAP consistency (Name, Address, Phone) across directories
- Citation accuracy (Yelp, Angi, HomeAdvisor, BBB)

**4. Competitor Gap Analysis**
- Top 3 competitors in their market: what they do better
- Keywords competitors rank for that client doesn't

**5. Review Analysis**
- Total count vs. top competitors
- Review response rate
- Sentiment themes (what customers praise vs. complain about)
