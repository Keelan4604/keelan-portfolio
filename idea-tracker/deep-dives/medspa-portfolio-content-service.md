**Score: 68** | Evaluated: 2026-04-10 (Evening Session)

## What It Is

A done-for-you content package specifically for med spas and aesthetic practices. Med spas live and die by before/after results content — it's their primary conversion tool on Instagram, Google, and their website. Most Alabama med spas either:
1. Post inconsistently (feast or famine)
2. Have no organized before/after portfolio on their website
3. Write weak captions with no storytelling

You deliver:
- 10-15 treatment case studies formatted for website portfolio section
- Monthly: 4 new case study posts with AI-written narrative (treatment, concern, result, CTA)
- Instagram caption writing for before/after photos they provide
- Optional: SEO-optimized treatment pages using case study content

## How It Works

**Client provides:** Before/after photos + rough treatment notes ("Patient had mild forehead lines, 20u Botox, happy with result at 2 weeks")

**Keelan delivers:**
1. Professional case study: Patient concern → Treatment selected → Why this treatment → Result + client quote (paraphrased)
2. Instagram caption: Engaging story, relevant hashtags, CTA to book
3. Website portfolio entry: SEO-friendly headline + full write-up

**Time per case study:** ~15-20 min with Claude assistance
**Time per month per client:** 2-3 hours total
