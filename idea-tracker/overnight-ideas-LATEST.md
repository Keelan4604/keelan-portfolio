## Key Market Insights (April 2026)

1. **AI is the multiplier, not the product.** The money is in applying AI to existing business problems (chatbots, automation, reporting), not in building AI tools. Businesses pay $5,000-$25,000+ for solutions that save 20+ hours/week.

2. **Recurring revenue > one-time projects.** Website sales at $500-$2,000 are fine, but the real play is monthly retainers ($200-$500/mo) for ongoing web presence + SEO + automation. 5 retainer clients = $1,000-$2,500/mo passive after setup.

3. **Cold outreach works but needs volume.** Expect 1-5% reply rates on cold email. Personalized demos (the current strategy) should outperform. Target 50-100 outreach per week for 1-5 meetings.

4. **Solo founders are thriving in micro-SaaS.** 36.3% of new startups are solo-founded (up from 23.7% in 2019). The pattern: ship fast, charge early, target niches larger competitors ignore.

5. **Trade-specific digital tools are massively underserved.** 89% of tradespeople lack adequate digital tools. This is directly in Keelan's path — website sales conversations reveal software pain points that become SaaS opportunities.

6. **US-person status is a competitive moat for engineering freelance.** ITAR-controlled projects require US persons, meaning offshore competition is eliminated and rates are 2-3x higher.
