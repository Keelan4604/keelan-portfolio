Research Highlights

UAV Propeller Acoustics (AERO)

Led design and computational analysis of four propeller geometries (planar, alular, toroidal, forward-swept) using SolidWorks CFD/FEA at 10,000 RPM, then validated with thrust and microphone testing.

Fixed-Wing eVTOL Development (VFS)

Designed and tested a fixed-wing VTOL platform with MATLAB transition-path modeling, actuation refinement, and phased flight validation for stability and control responsiveness.
