# GBP Optimization — Deliverables Checklist ($199 Service)

**Internal delivery guide. Follow this checklist for every GBP optimization job.**

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Work Audit

Complete before making any changes. This is your baseline.

- [ ] Screenshot the current GBP listing (search results view and full profile)
- [ ] Note current Google search ranking for primary keyword + city (e.g., "plumber Raleigh")
- [ ] Check if the business is verified on Google — if not, flag immediately (verification is a prerequisite)
- [ ] Review existing reviews: count, average rating, any unanswered reviews
- [ ] Check for duplicate listings — search business name and phone number
- [ ] Note any Google warnings, suspensions, or suggested edits pending
- [ ] Pull up the top 3 local competitors' GBP profiles for comparison (Phase 7)
- [ ] Ask the client for: logo, best photos they have, list of all services, service area, hours, and any seasonal changes

---

## Phase 2: Profile Completeness

Go through every field. Nothing should be blank.

### Core Info
- [ ] **Business name** — matches real-world name exactly (no keyword stuffing)
- [ ] **Primary category** — most specific category available (e.g., "Emergency Plumber" not just "Plumber")
- [ ] **Secondary categories** — add 3-5 relevant secondary categories
- [ ] **Address** — correct, formatted consistently with website and other listings
- [ ] **Service area** — defined if applicable (service-area businesses vs. storefront)
- [ ] **Phone number** — local number preferred over toll-free
- [ ] **Website URL** — links to correct page (homepage or relevant landing page)
- [ ] **Hours of operation** — accurate, including special hours for holidays if applicable
- [ ] **Opening date** — filled in

### Extended Info
- [ ] **Business description** — rewrite to be keyword-rich, locally relevant, and compelling (750 characters max, use all of it)
- [ ] **Services / Menu section** — populate with all services offered, with descriptions and prices if available
- [ ] **Attributes** — check all applicable (women-owned, veteran-owned, wheelchair accessible, etc.)
- [ ] **Appointment URL** — add if they take online bookings
- [ ] **Products** — add if applicable

---

## Phase 3: Photo Strategy

Google rewards profiles with photos. Businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks.

### Recommendations to give the client:

| Photo Type | Quantity | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Logo | 1 | 720x720px minimum, PNG preferred |
| Cover photo | 1 | 1080x608px, best representation of the business |
| Interior shots | 3-5 | Well-lit, clean, show the space |
| Exterior / storefront | 2-3 | Daytime, clearly shows signage |
| Team / staff | 2-4 | Friendly, professional, builds trust |
| Work samples / products | 5-10 | Before/after for contractors, plated food for restaurants, etc. |
| Action shots | 2-3 | Team doing the work, serving customers |

### Photo guidelines to share:
- Minimum 720px wide, aim for 1200px+
- JPEG or PNG format
- No stock photos — Google can detect and deprioritize them
- No heavy filters or text overlays
- Geotagged if possible (taken on-site with a phone)

- [ ] Upload any photos the client provides
- [ ] Write up a photo recommendation list for shots they still need

---

## Phase 4: Google Posts (5 Posts)

Create and publish 5 posts. Each post needs an image (can use client photos or relevant Unsplash images as placeholder until client provides).

| Post # | Type | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Welcome / About** | Introduce the business. Who they are, how long they've been serving the area, what makes them different. |
| 2 | **Service Highlight #1** | Feature their most popular or highest-margin service. Include a call to action (call, book, visit). |
| 3 | **Service Highlight #2** | Feature a secondary service, especially one people might not know they offer. |
| 4 | **Seasonal / Promo** | Tie into current season or a limited-time offer. Creates urgency. (e.g., "Spring AC tune-ups — book now before summer rush") |
| 5 | **Review Spotlight** | Highlight a strong customer review. Quote it, thank the customer, reinforce the service. |

### Post best practices:
- 150-300 words per post
- Include a CTA button (Call, Learn More, Book, Order)
- Use relevant keywords naturally
- Posts expire after 6 months — note this in delivery report

- [ ] Post 1 published
- [ ] Post 2 published
- [ ] Post 3 published
- [ ] Post 4 published
- [ ] Post 5 published

---

## Phase 5: Q&A Section

Populate the Q&A with 8-10 common questions and answers. You ask the question from one Google account and answer from the business profile. This controls the narrative and pre-empts real customer questions.

### Standard questions to populate (customize per business):

1. What are your hours of operation?
2. Do you offer free estimates / consultations?
3. What areas do you serve?
4. Do you offer emergency / same-day service?
5. What forms of payment do you accept?
6. Are you licensed and insured?
7. How do I schedule an appointment?
8. What is your most popular service?
9. Do you offer any discounts (senior, military, first-time customer)?
10. What sets you apart from other [business type] in the area?

- [ ] All 8-10 Q&As posted and answered
- [ ] Answers include relevant keywords naturally
- [ ] Answers are helpful and specific (not generic filler)

---

## Phase 6: Review Response Templates

Provide the client with copy-paste templates they can use going forward.

### Positive review response templates (provide 3-4 variations):

**Template 1:**
> Thank you so much, [Name]! We're glad you had a great experience with [specific service if mentioned]. We appreciate your trust in [Business Name] and look forward to serving you again!

**Template 2:**
> [Name], this made our day! Our team works hard to [relevant value — deliver quality, provide fast service, etc.], and it means a lot to hear it's paying off. Thank you for choosing us!

**Template 3:**
> We really appreciate you taking the time to leave this review, [Name]. Customers like you are the reason we love what we do. See you next time!

### Negative review response templates (provide 2-3 variations):

**Template 1:**
> [Name], thank you for your feedback. We're sorry your experience didn't meet expectations. We'd like to make this right — please reach out to us directly at [phone/email] so we can address your concerns.

**Template 2:**
> We appreciate you letting us know, [Name]. This isn't the standard we hold ourselves to, and we want to resolve this. Please contact us at [phone/email] at your earliest convenience.

**Template 3:**
> [Name], we take this seriously and want to understand what happened. Please reach out to [owner name] directly at [phone/email]. We're committed to making this right.

- [ ] Positive templates written (3-4)
- [ ] Negative templates written (2-3)
- [ ] Templates customized with business name and contact info

## Phase 7: Competitor Analysis

Brief comparison against the top 3 local competitors on Google Maps.

For each competitor, note:

- [ ] **Competitor 1:** _______________
  - Review count and average rating
  - Profile completeness (description, hours, categories, photos)
  - Number of Google Posts
  - Q&A populated?
  - Website quality (quick glance)
  - Strengths to learn from
  - Weaknesses our client can exploit

- [ ] **Competitor 2:** _______________
  - (Same checklist as above)

- [ ] **Competitor 3:** _______________
  - (Same checklist as above)

### Summary to include in delivery:
- Where the client now stands vs. competitors after optimization
- Specific advantages gained
- Remaining gaps (often leads to upsell conversations)

- [ ] Photo recommendations (what they still need to provide)
- [ ] Review response templates
- [ ] Competitor comparison summary
- [ ] Next steps and recommendations

### Live changes already made:
- [ ] Profile fields updated
- [ ] Google Posts published
- [ ] Q&A section populated

### Delivery meeting (15-20 min call or in-person):
- Walk through the PDF report
- Show the live changes on Google
- Answer questions
- Transition into upsell conversation (Phase 9)
