## Key Patterns From This Session

**Pattern 1: Med spas are a better version of the contractor vertical.**
Contractors (score: 72-74 in session 2) and med spas (score: 68-74 this session) score nearly identically. But med spas have higher budgets, stronger content needs (before/after = product proof), and are more likely to pay $400/mo for a done-for-you system vs. a contractor who haggles over $200. All existing services (website, GBP, chatbot, portfolio content) work directly in this vertical. **Consider adding med spas to the primary lead gen target list.**

**Pattern 2: Upwork AI freelancing is the fastest new track to activate.**
Website sales requires finding leads, building demos, waiting for responses. Upwork AI freelancing requires: creating a profile. At 178% YoY demand growth and supply bottlenecked by engineering credibility, this is the fastest path to a second income stream. Zero competition with the primary track.

**Pattern 3: The contractor portfolio case study service (session 2, score 73) translates perfectly to med spas.**
Don't build a new service. Reframe the existing "contractor case study" offering as "med spa treatment portfolio" — same Claude workflow, same pricing model, better buyers. One service, two verticals.

**Pattern 4: Saturation kills obvious AI agency plays every time.**
Instagram DM automation: killed by ManyChat brand recognition. Restaurant social media: killed by every marketing student doing it. Review response automation: killed by Reviewly and built-in Google AI. The only ideas that survive saturation scoring are those requiring either (a) local physical presence, or (b) technical engineering credibility that 95% of "AI agency" people don't have.
