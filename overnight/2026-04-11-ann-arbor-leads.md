## Key Insight

**Ann Arbor/Ypsilanti is more digitally mature than Tuscaloosa.** Finding no-website businesses is harder here. The opportunity may be more about **website upgrades** (Square → custom, dated templates → modern) rather than pure no-website targeting.

**Ypsilanti is the goldmine** — more blue-collar, more independent shops, more digital gaps. Focus lead gen there, not Ann Arbor proper.

**The in-person advantage will be huge.** When Keelan arrives in August, walking into these businesses with an iPad showing their demo will convert better than any email. These are relationship-driven communities.
