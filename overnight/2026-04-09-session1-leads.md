## Key Patterns Observed

### Strongest Pitch Angles (in order of effectiveness)
1. **Concrete site problems** - Jordan's spam footer, Breeland Booth's placeholder text, Crimson Carpet's 403 error
2. **No website at all** - Fence Crafters Alabama (45 years, zero web presence)
3. **Awards/reviews not shown on site** - Garage Doors 4 U (9 Super Service Awards), Bama Roofing (67 reviews)
4. **Decades of experience hidden** - Multiple 30-45+ year businesses on generic templates

### Hibu is everywhere
At least 5 businesses are on Hibu/DM templates: Roofing Alabama, Olympic Fence, Breeland Booth, JD ESCO, Hicks Plumbing. These are the easiest to pitch against — they all look identical.

### Birmingham is 10x the opportunity
Tuscaloosa has maybe 5-8 strong prospects per session. Birmingham has dozens per industry. The drive is ~1 hour but the market density is dramatically higher.

### No-email businesses need phone calls or drive-bys
5 of the 13 drafted emails have no delivery email. These are some of the BEST prospects (Fence Crafters 77, South's Finest 74, Bama Roofing 72). Worth a phone call or physical visit.
